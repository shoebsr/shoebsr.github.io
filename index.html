About Me

The Developer Behind The Code

I'm Abdul Shoeb — a Senior Full Stack .NET developer based in Hyderabad, with 5.5+ years building secure, data-intensive enterprise applications across government, healthcare and loyalty/rewards domains.

At Schoolnet India I own the architecture and delivery of LinQ Pulse, the analytics platform for a government skilling programme — 7 role levels, 21,000+ learners, 18 states. I work in .NET 8, ASP.NET Core, Angular, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Redis and AWS, applying Clean Architecture, CQRS and MediatR in production.

"The model decides the layout — the application computes the numbers. AI that stays accurate, auditable and secure."

I've integrated Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and Groq into business dashboards through a secure server-side AI gateway, and I'm now building an agentic tool-calling layer. Experienced with PII-sensitive data, role-based access, JWT/SSO and DPDP Act 2023 compliance. Currently pursuing AWS Solutions Architect – Associate on the path to Solution Architect.
