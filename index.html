About Me

The Developer Behind The Code

I'm Abdul Shoeb — a full-stack .NET + Angular developer from Amravati, Maharashtra. I turn complex business requirements into clean, scalable software. From government EdTech platforms serving thousands of students, to hospital systems managing critical healthcare data across Saudi Arabia.

My engineering philosophy is simple: architecture first, features second. Every line I write is backed by SOLID principles, Clean Architecture, and the relentless question — "will this scale at 10x?"

"Not just a developer who writes code — an engineer who designs systems that outlast deadlines, survive team changes, and still perform under pressure."

Currently deep-diving into System Design, AWS Solutions Architecture, and Distributed Systems — my structured path toward Solution Architect by 2027. Side projects include masjid management apps, voter list tools, and community utilities built for real people with real problems.
